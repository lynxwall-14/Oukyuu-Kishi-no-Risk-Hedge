# Translation Frame - 王宮騎士のリスクヘッジ (The Royal Knight's Risk Hedge)

- 世界観 (Theme / setting): Comedic adult fantasy, an event-collection H-game. After slaying a demon, a stoic female Royal Knight spends four days in a remote, temporarily all-male bathhouse village, trying to avoid pregnancy "accidents" while her poison-immunity potion has disabled contraception.
- 時代感 (Era / technology level): Generic medieval-fantasy kingdom (king, knights, potions, enchanted armor, monsters), plus bath amenities the text itself treats as unusually modern (hot showers, a sauna).
- 文体方針 (Register policy): Plain, modern, readable English. Deadpan situational comedy played straight. Courteous formality for the heroine against coarse villagers, bandits and bathers. Explicit content rendered explicitly. Literary third-person past-tense narration.
- 固有名詞方針 (Naming policy): Give the two named women natural Western spellings (Lorentia, Sazanna). Keep the village name transliterated (Seseragi). Translate titles, institutions, places and descriptive NPC nameplates into idiomatic English. Monster names already built from English loanwords stay in English form (Skeleton Worm).
