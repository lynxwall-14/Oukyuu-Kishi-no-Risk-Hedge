# Game Characters
ロレンチア (Lorentia) - Female, 26, human (status screen). Protagonist and Actor 1, with a fixed name. A Royal Knight specialised in combat, sent to slay the Viper Demon. Voice: formal, courteous, very even. Short declarative sentences, polite to everyone including the bandits. Dry, deadpan understatement ("...What a rude chair." CE9). Never uses the men's slang: in sex scenes and anatomy she stays clinical (breasts, genitals, intercourse, ejaculate inside, fertilization). Terse mission calls: 任務遂行。 -> "Mission accomplished." (Map002), 確保！ -> "Secured!" (CE8), 掃討完了 -> "Sweep complete." なさい commands are stern: 覚悟なさい -> "Prepare yourself." (Map002). Interjections: おや -> "Oh?", …コホン -> "*Ahem*". Her only laugh is …ふふ -> "...Fufu." (CE17). Parenthesised inner thoughts stay polite. Sazanna calls her 鉄仮面 ("stone-faced", CE17). The personality toggle (bashful vs unflappable) changes only a few narration and thought lines; her voice stays the same in both modes.
サザンナ (Sazanna) - Female; Royal Knight and pharmacology/potion specialist. Lorentia's colleague and close friend; she brewed the poison-resistance potion. Labelled ？？？ before her first prologue line. Voice: teasing, theatrical, ladylike big-sister speech (わよ/わね/かしら/ではなくて), bubbly exclamations, drawn-out vowels. Interjections: あーら -> "Oh my~", ああん -> "Aww~", あらあら -> "My, my", あらら -> "Oh dear", でもでも -> "But, but". Example: "My, my, isn't that a serious problem!?" (Map011 EV3). Calls Lorentia ロレンチアちゃん as a tease. Keep the CE17 win/lose pair: 私の勝ちね！ -> "Then I win!" answered by 私の負けでいいですよ -> "Fine, I lose."
村長 (Village Chief) - Male; chief of Seseragi Village, newly in office after the Viper Demon killed his two predecessors. The source says only that he looks young (お若く見えた); his age is never stated, so do not add age or youth descriptors beyond the source. Pronoun ボク. Voice: earnest, flustered, polite. Stammers his greetings (ろ、ロレンチアさん！？ -> "L-Lorentia-san!?"), apologises a lot. Interjections: うぁ！？, ふぇ！？, あいたた -> "Ow, ow...". Does not know what ejaculation is and calls it おしっこ (see quirks).
族長 (Bandit Boss) - Male; leader of the bandits who raid the village (narration: 山賊の長らしき大男). His men address him as お頭 / 親分 / 族長 -> "Boss". Voice: coarse, swaggering, mocking (俺/俺サマ, アンタ/お前, ～ぜ/じゃねーか). Sneers 王宮騎士サマ -> "Miss Royal Knight". Laugh ガハハ -> "Gahaha!"; the offscreen ？？？ 「ガハハハハ！」 in Map008 EV3 is him. Syllable-split taunt ひ・と・じ・ち -> "Hos-ta-ges."
山賊 (Bandit) - Male bandit underlings. Rowdy; laughs ギャハハ -> "Gyahaha!", ゲヘヘ -> "Gehehe". One talks like a flunky (ッス) and garbles 王宮騎士 as おーきゅうきし -> "Roy-ull Knight-thingy?" (Map003 EV15).
山賊たち (Bandits) - Group nameplate for the bandit underlings.
毒蛇魔人 (Viper Demon) - Gender unknown. The demon that poisoned and razed the village; summons vipers; slain in its temple (Map002). Voice: polite, smug villain (私, ふむ, ～ようです, いかがでしょう？) that collapses into comic panic: そ、そんな馬鹿なー！！！！ -> "Th-this can't be!!!!"
毒蛇魔神 (Viper Demon) - Source slip for 毒蛇魔人, seen only in the Map002 display name. Same entity.
毒蛇 (Viper) - The Viper Demon's summoned venomous snakes (also 蛇達, 下級の毒蛇 "lesser vipers", 警備用の蛇達 "guard snakes"). Their lines are only growls (ガルルア！, グガガ！).
鍛冶屋 (Blacksmith) - Male; village blacksmith and shop owner (Lorentia addresses him as 店主さん). Voice: nervous, over-polite merchant speech out loud (申し訳ございません), crude inner thoughts in parentheses. Recurring 一生分 -> "a lifetime's worth" (CE2, CE14).
浮浪者 (Vagrant) - Male old man who lost everything to the vipers; lecherous. Voice: folksy old-man speech (ワシ, じゃ/のう/かの/ぞい) -> grumbling, rustic English. Laughs: ふへへ -> "Fuhehe", ふひひ -> "Fuhihi", へっへ -> "Heh heh". 絶景かな -> "What a view, what a view!" (kabuki quote, CE13).
覗き魔 (Peeping Tom) - Male; follows and spies on Lorentia (CE10, CE11, Map001 EV24). Voice: servile and cringing (ですぜ, へい, えへへ) -> "Y-yes, ma'am!". The source wavers between the pronouns ワシ and 俺; use a plain "I" throughout.
挙動不審な男 (Shifty Man) - Male; the Map001 EV6 man who claims to be "just picking wild vegetables". Possibly the later Peeping Tom (unconfirmed); keep a separate label, and do not reuse "Suspicious" (reserved for 不審者).

# Worldbuilding
王宮騎士のリスクヘッジ (The Royal Knight's Risk Hedge) - Game title.
王宮騎士 (Royal Knight) - The King's direct-command unit of specialists, founded to fight bandits and monsters ("the Royal Knights"). Also each member's title, and Lorentia's class.
女騎士 (lady knight) - Generic description of Lorentia, used in the ending gossip line.
王都 (Royal Capital) - The capital at the centre of the continent.

戦士ギルド (Warriors' Guild)
セセラギ村 (Seseragi Village) - The eastern village with the bathhouse. Seseragi means "babbling brook", but no line plays on the meaning, so keep the transliteration.
西の林 (West Woods) - Lorentia's patrol woods; keep distinct from 東の森.
東の森 (East Forest) - Where the guard corps goes on Day 2, leaving the village open to the bandits.
毒蛇魔神の神殿 (Temple of the Viper Demon) - Map002 display name. The dialogue says 毒蛇魔人の神殿; do not render 魔神 as "Devil" here.
スケルトンワーム (Skeleton Worm) - Translucent, worm-like water monster that lays eggs inside females. The eggs are expelled without implanting if the host is already pregnant: this is the "risk hedge" of the title.
毒耐性ポーション (Poison Resistance Potion) - Sazanna's potion. It grants full poison and corrosion immunity for "about six months", but also stops contraceptives from working.
腐食液 (corrosive fluid) - The vipers' spit, which dissolves armor.
